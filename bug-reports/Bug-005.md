# BUG-005 - Senior discount is 10% instead of the required 5%

## Severity
Medium

## Priority
High

## Environment
- Browser: Chrome Version 155.0.8059.40
- OS: Windows 11 Home x64
- Page: Checkout

## Steps to reproduce
1. Open the Demo Shop main page.
2. Add any product to the cart.
3. Proceed to Checkout.
4. Enter a date of birth for a customer who is 65 years old or older.
5. Complete the required checkout fields.
6. Check the Order Summary.

## Expected result
Customers aged 65 or older should receive a 5% discount on the entire order.

For an order with a subtotal of $3000.00, the discount should be:

- Senior Discount (5%): $150.00
- Total: $2850.00

## Actual result
The system applies a 10% senior discount instead of the required 5%.

For an order with a subtotal of $3000.00, the Order Summary displays:

- Senior Discount (10%): $300.00
- Total: $2700.00
